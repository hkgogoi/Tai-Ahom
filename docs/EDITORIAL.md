# Editorial policy

Release 0.2, 26 October 2026.

1. Preserve the original witness and source location. An image index, PDF page and manuscript folio are different fields.
2. Preserve original source IDs, including any source numbering peculiarities. A project ID is not an archive shelfmark.
3. Keep inherited transcriptions separate from independent visual readings. This release contains no newly certified visual transcription.
4. Preserve corresponding English as supplied. Do not silently replace an unresolved gloss or an uncertain translation.
5. Keep editorial reconstruction and historical interpretation in separate nullable fields. Both remain empty for imported record readings in this release.
6. Retain original source objects, notes and alternatives in the private research package. Legacy strings remain evidence, not Unicode display substitutes.
7. A Unicode conversion is an encoding intervention. It does not establish a new translation or remove uncertainty.
8. Source-wide subject tags facilitate discovery. They are not evidence that each entry contains that subject.

## Bar Amra

Tai-Ahom headwords, the defining formula, Assamese explanations written in Ahom script, romanization and English lexical glosses are separate fields. Preserve conflicting readings; do not normalize them away.

The revised master has 1,236 recovered records: 1,230 original objects unchanged and six additions. Of the additions, four are partial; two are full published source entries. B and C are inherited record-level assessments. Four A-level headword checks do not certify their whole entries.

The folio register contains 61 supplied images. Four folios have no accepted transcription: 1r, 10r, 26r and 26v. Fifty-five numbered entry IDs remain without accepted readings. The 65-target research register includes these 59 outstanding targets and the six additions. Never invent entry counts for whole-folio gaps.

EAP373/26/2 canvas 3 was matched to 1r and canvas 21 to 10r in the prior review. No replacement photograph for 26r or 26v was established. Other Bar Amra witnesses must not be inserted as if they were the same manuscript.

## Six other editions

The six editions contribute 3,401 numbered records. Their English readings and uncertainty markers are preserved. They are labelled `unreviewed` at the new archive's whole-entry level because the Bar Amra B/C scheme has not been applied to them.

Lakni is partial. Kham Kau Phi Dam is an oral ritual text. Nemi entry 1653 has no recovered Ahom text. Pvn entry 536 is a source placeholder. Ma Likha entries 95, 183, 187, 201 and 300 retain `[unresolved-50]`; entries 120 and 265 have no standalone source translation. Do not resolve these using guessed text.

## Review procedure

For a future correction, record the witness, folio, source entry, exact old value, proposed value, evidence, reviewer and date. Preserve the old reading as an alternative. Independently review reproduction rights before moving any full text or image into the public dataset. A successful software test is not manuscript certification.
